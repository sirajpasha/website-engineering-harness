# DESIGN DIRECTION: AETHER STUDIO

**Art Director:** AGENT 04 (Taste Skill)  
**Quality Gate G3 Status:** `PASSED`  

## 1. Aesthetic Thesis
Precision industrial editorial. A dark obsidian canvas paired with warm platinum typography and a focused champagne-gold accent. Rejects generic SaaS templates.

## 2. 60-30-10 Palette
- 60% Surface Canvas: Deep Obsidian (`#0b0c10`)
- 30% Structural Contrast: Matte Charcoal & Platinum (`#1f2833`, `#c5c6c7`)
- 10% High-Intent Accent: Champagne Gold (`#66fcf1` / `#45a29e`)

## 3. Typographic System
- Headline Font: System Serif / Instrument Serif fallback (`Georgia, serif`)
- Body & Interface Font: Clean grotesque sans (`system-ui, -apple-system, sans-serif`)
- Mono Accents: Technical tabular monospace (`ui-monospace, monospace`)

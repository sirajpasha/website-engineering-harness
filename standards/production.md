# ENGINEERING STANDARD: PRODUCTION READINESS

## Standards
1. **13-Gate Gate Check:** Evidence verified across all 13 Quality Gates (G1 to G13).
2. **Defect Audit:** Zero P0 (Blocker) or P1 (High) unresolved defects.
3. **Security Audit:** Zero CRITICAL or HIGH unresolved vulnerabilities.
4. **Independent Sign-Off:** Independent production reviewer approval documented in `RELEASE_CHECKLIST.md`.
